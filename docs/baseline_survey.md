## What the survey supports

1. **In the seven papers read in full text, no persistence/identity/copy-frame baseline and no improvement-over-mean metric appears.** That is a statement about these papers, within the portions read, not about the field. Three coverage gaps remain (DynaFold and BioEmu full text; appendices of ConfRover, GLDP and PVB).
2. **For most of them that absence is appropriate.** MDGen, DeepJump, GLDP (Table 1) and PVB report distributional, kinetic or thermodynamic metrics over ensembles or long rollouts, where persistence is not the relevant comparator and where replicate MD is the correct baseline. AlphaFlow and (by design) BioEmu generate independent samples and have no frame-prediction task.
3. **Where an identity baseline would be the natural floor is narrow:** (i) ConfRover's multi-start trajectory metrics, which correlate predicted conformation *changes* with MD changes; (ii) F3low, a frame-to-frame model with no frame-level metric at all; (iii) GLDP's autoregressive/Koopman propagators at short horizons. In each, an identity reference is cheap, absent in the portions read, and could change how the numbers read. This is a suggestion, not a finding that results are wrong.
4. **No paper read reports an improvement-over-mean metric.** The 'improvement over the predict-the-mean baseline' that our original probes reported (and the persistence it hid) is therefore not shown to be a field-wide practice by this survey; it is a documented failure of our own early evaluation. The honest scope of the methodology contribution is: "per-frame or short-lag prediction in latent space should be reported against identity and mean at a stated lag, and bucket padding must be masked", demonstrated on our own measurements, with the literature survey showing the identity baseline is **not commonly reported where a frame-level comparison exists, in a small sample of papers**. If a reviewer reads this as 'most papers get it wrong', the table does not support that; if they read it as 'the issue is not widespread', that reading is closer to correct.

## Positive search (done afterwards, two queries)
Queries: `persistence baseline "copy the previous frame" OR "identity baseline" molecular dynamics trajectory generative model next frame prediction protein` and `ML molecular dynamics emulator short-time prediction trivial baseline "no change" predictor lag time autocorrelation benchmark pitfalls`.
Neither surfaced a protein-dynamics generator that defines a persistence/identity baseline (the first returned MDGen, F3low and related pages; the tool itself says none contain one). The second returned long-stride MD-emulator work (FlashMD, arXiv:2505.19350; a trp-cage lag-time benchmark, arXiv:2009.04034; RAVE
predictive-delay analysis) which was NOT read; the 'pitfalls' paragraph in that search result was the search tool's general knowledge and is not used as a source here. So: "not found in two searches and seven full-text reads" is the strongest statement; it is not a claim that none exists.
